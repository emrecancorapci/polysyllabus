A resistor and a capacitor in series form a simple filter. The output is taken across the capacitor.

<Figure caption="A series RC network. The output is taken across the capacitor.">
  ![A source drives a resistor R in series with a capacitor C to ground](./fig-rc.svg)
</Figure>

## Cutoff frequency

The cutoff frequency is where the output power drops to half of its low frequency value. For this filter it is:

$$
f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi RC}
$$

With $R = 1\,\text{k}\Omega$ and $C = 100\,\text{nF}$, the cutoff frequency is about 1591.5 Hz.

The time constant is $\tau = RC$. Here it is 0.1 ms.

## Why high frequencies are attenuated

A capacitor has a lower impedance at higher frequencies. At high frequencies it acts almost like a short circuit, so very little voltage appears across it.
