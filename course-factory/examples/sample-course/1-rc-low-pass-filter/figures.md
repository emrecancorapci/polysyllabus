## fig-rc

- Shows: the series RC low-pass network of the lesson.
- Elements: an ideal voltage source on the left, resistor `R` in series, capacitor `C` to ground, output marked across the capacitor.
- Labels: source, `R`, `C`, `V_in` at the input node, `V_out` at the output node.
- Current direction: conventional current flows from the source through `R` into `C`.
- Polarity: `V_out` is measured from the top plate of `C` to ground.
- Alt text: "A voltage source drives a resistor R in series with a capacitor C to ground".
