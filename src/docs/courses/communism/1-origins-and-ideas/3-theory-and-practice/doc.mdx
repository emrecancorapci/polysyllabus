## States that invoked the theory

Later states that ruled *in communism's name*, the Soviet Union most influentially, made choices Marx never specified: one-party rule, centralized planning, suppression of dissent. Evaluating the theory separately from these implementations is a basic move in the literature.

<CollapsibleSolution title="Check yourself: the two classes">
  In Marx's model of capitalism, name the two central classes and what distinguishes them. The **bourgeoisie** own the means of production. The **proletariat** own only their labor, which they sell for wages.
