## Energy conservation around a loop

Energy is conserved around any closed loop: the voltages raised by sources equal the voltages dropped across loads.

$$
\sum_{\text{loop}} V = 0
$$

The sign convention takes a voltage as you pass its polarity: rises positive, drops negative. The reverse also works, as long as you are consistent around the loop.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Pick one direction">
  Walk the loop in one direction and stick to it. Mixing directions halfway through is the most common source of sign errors.
</Callout>

## Key takeaways

- KVL: energy conservation around a loop, $\sum V = 0$.
- A consistent sign convention matters more than which convention you pick.
