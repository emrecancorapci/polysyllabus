## The Bode view

A Bode plot shows the same story in the frequency domain. Gain is flat at low frequency, then rolls off at $-20\,\text{dB/decade}$ past $f_c$. The phase swings from $0°$ to $-90°$. Both panels share the same logarithmic frequency axis, so features line up vertically.

## Explore it yourself

Drag the sliders and watch the cutoff and the whole magnitude curve move. The formulas are exactly the ones above: $f_c = 1/(2\pi R C)$ and $|H| = 1/\sqrt{1 + (f/f_c)^2}$.

## Key takeaways

- Past $f_c$ the gain falls at $-20\,\text{dB/decade}$ and the phase approaches $-90°$.
- The Bode magnitude and phase panels share the frequency axis by construction.
