## Charging through the resistor

Switch the source on and the capacitor charges through the resistor with time constant $\tau = RC$. For $R = 1\,\text{k}\Omega$ and $C = 100\,\text{nF}$, $\tau = 0.1\,\text{ms}$. After roughly $5\tau$ the capacitor is essentially fully charged.

<Callout variant="note" title="Five time constants">
  The $5\tau$ rule of thumb is where the exponential reaches $99.3\%$ of its final value. That is close enough by engineering standards.
</Callout>

## Key takeaways

- In time, the RC circuit charges with $\tau = RC$.
- After $5\tau$ the exponential has reached 99.3 percent of its final value.
